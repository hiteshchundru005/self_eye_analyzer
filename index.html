Instructions for Best Results:

  1. Position yourself in good, even lighting (avoid shadows on your face)
  2. Sit about 30-50cm from your camera
  3. Remove glasses (unless you're testing with them on purpose)
  4. Keep your head straight and look directly at the camera
  5. Hold still during capture and analysis

Note: This is not a substitute for professional eye care. Results are estimates only.
